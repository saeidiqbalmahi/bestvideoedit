What you get

Everything a YouTube video needs to retain viewers

A good YouTube edit is not just cutting out pauses. It is pacing, story, sound and captions working together so the viewer never feels a reason to click away.

🎯

Retention-first cutting

Dead air, filler words, repeated takes and slow intros are cut. The pace stays tight from the hook to the end screen, so average view duration — the number YouTube rewards most — goes up instead of flatlining after 30 seconds.

🪝

Hooks and intros that hold

We open with the strongest moment of your video, then pay it off. Pattern interrupts, punch-ins and on-screen text keep the first minute moving, because the first minute decides whether the rest gets watched at all.

🎬

B-roll, captions and graphics

Relevant B-roll over talking sections, animated captions in your brand font, lower-thirds, callouts and smooth transitions. Your video looks produced, not recorded — without looking over-edited.

🎨

Colour and sound design

Clean colour grading, balanced voice levels, background music mixed low enough to never fight your voice, and noise cleanup. Your audio is checked the way a viewer hears it — on a phone, on a laptop, on earbuds.

📑

Chapters, end screens and thumbnail brief

Chapters so viewers can jump to what they want, an end screen that points to your next video, and a thumbnail brief — the frame, the expression, the text — so your click-through rate gets the same attention as your edit.

⚡

Shorts cut from every video

Every long video can become 5–10 Shorts, Reels and TikToks, cut from its best moments with captions and a hook in the first 2 seconds. One recording, two formats, twice the reach.

Do I get the same editor every time?

Yes. Every monthly plan includes a dedicated editor who learns your style, fonts, colours and pacing. Per-video clients get the same editor on repeat orders whenever possible.

What if I don't like the first edit?

Comment with timestamps and we revise it. Starter plans include 2 revision rounds; Growth and Scale include unlimited revisions. The free test edit exists so you can check our style before spending anything.

Do you make thumbnails too?

We write a thumbnail brief with every edit — the frame, the expression and the text that will earn the click — and Growth plans include thumbnails and hooks strategy. Full thumbnail design is included in the monthly art-direction work.

What music do you use?

Licensed music, mixed low under your voice. Tell us if you have a track, a licensed library, or a sound you want — or let us choose. We avoid tracks that have caused copyright claims before.
